# ADR-0004: Sunday research within the existing hourly watch

**Decision (2026-11-19, documented 2026-11-22):** User restored a full six-module Chinese financial-risk and asset research report in the **Sunday 10:00 Beijing-time hour** of the already enabled hourly ChatGPT condition-watch. The old standalone `金融危机周报` task remains disabled. The weekly research branch is an exception to ordinary non-alert silence, **not** permission to relax four emergency gates. Preserve original source dates, six-module unknown grades where insufficient, original historical Nov 8 orange/70 without comparing it to technical green, and the bounded Sunday catch-up/weekly duplicate review.

**Supersession:** [ADR-0003](ADR-0003.md) remains the correct historical 2026-11-09 decision to disable unsolicited standalone weekly pushes and avoid unverified scores; its *ban on every Sunday research presentation* is superseded by this later explicit user instruction. Its independent official-research and non-notification controls remain binding.

**Delivery truth:** A ChatGPT answer, the platform's scheduled-run completion, a GitHub candidate Issue and `data/weekly_baseline.json` are distinct records. Without verifiable terminal acknowledgement, do not claim exact-once delivery. The baseline may be written only when the full six-module evidence is independently verified and GitHub safe-write rights exist; absence of baseline update does not by itself prove no ChatGPT report. See [Issue #8](https://github.com/lican1213/financial-crisis-early-warning/issues/8) and [cadence](../../docs/operations/CADENCE.md).

**Engineering choice:** Use the existing hourly task and MOC; no new schedule, webhook, configuration framework, scored predictor, data store or duplicate automation.
